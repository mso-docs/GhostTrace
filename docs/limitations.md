# Limits of interpretation

Persona names describe experimental instructions. A response may express regret without establishing a feeling. Distance is descriptive, not causal. Decodable condition information could reflect literal wording, sequence length, token positions or generic instruction following. There is no psychological ground truth.

This is a small synthetic English pilot on a small instruction-tuned model. Families share structure; two held-out families are not a broad benchmark. Backend numerical differences, human-rating uncertainty, bundled behavior instructions and selected pooling/middle-block rules constrain interpretation. Large representation effects may not correspond to behavior. A null result is a successful outcome.

No live-model smoke, full pilot, blinded human ratings, real replay bundle or hardware performance has been validated merely by installing the application. Development fixtures must remain labeled illustrative. No illustrative values may be exported as measured evidence. Confirmatory use requires reviewed text, immutable model revisions, a frozen preregistration and successful deterministic preflight.

Optional PCA must fit one projection on development only. Probes detect condition information, not remorse. Activation patching is out of scope until aligned interventions and self/random/reverse controls are implemented.
